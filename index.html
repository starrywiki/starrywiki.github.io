About Me

I am Anqi Li (李安琪), an undergraduate studying Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I am part of the Zhiyuan Honors Program (JOHN Class). I expect to graduate in June 2028.

I am currently a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia, mentored by Hui Xue, working on multi-agent automated research and synthetic-data strategies. I also conduct research in Yulun Zhang’s group at SJTU, focusing on efficient vision-language model inference and multimodal reasoning.

I am interested in making multimodal AI more efficient, capable, and practical. Feel free to reach out at starrywiki@sjtu.edu.cn.

Research Interests

  • Efficient Vision-Language Models. Token pruning, reasoning token control, and inference optimization.
  • Multimodal Reasoning. Complex visual understanding and grounded reasoning in vision-language models.
  • AI Systems. Multi-agent automated research, synthetic data, and scale-aware evaluation.
